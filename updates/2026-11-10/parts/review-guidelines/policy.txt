**1. Purpose**

Pitchside Forum welcomes honest reviews and discussions about online casinos, sportsbooks and gambling platforms.

Our goal is to provide a place where players can share their experiences, evaluate operators and help other members make more informed decisions.

Both positive and negative reviews are welcome. We encourage balanced, evidence-based contributions and open discussion.

**2. Who Can Submit a Review?**

Any registered member may submit a review of a casino or sportsbook.

However:

- Reviews should be based on genuine personal experiences.
- Members should have actually used the service they are reviewing.
- Reviews should contain meaningful information rather than unsupported ratings.
- Members should not review a business solely to promote or damage its reputation.
- Reviews based on information from other sources must clearly identify that information.

Members who have not personally used an operator may still participate in discussions, but should not present second-hand information as a personal review.

**3. What Should a Review Include?**

To make reviews useful, members are encouraged to cover the following:

- **Operator:** Name of the casino or sportsbook.
- **Website:** Official website address.
- **Overall experience:** General impression of the service.
- **Deposits and withdrawals:** Payment methods, processing times and any difficulties experienced.
- **Customer support:** Responsiveness and quality of assistance.
- **Bonuses and promotions:** Whether advertised offers matched the actual terms.
- **Sportsbook experience:** Markets, odds, bet settlement and platform performance, where applicable.
- **Casino experience:** Games, software providers and gameplay experience, where applicable.
- **Account verification:** Whether KYC procedures were straightforward or problematic.
- **Final rating:** Overall assessment and reasons for the score.

Not every review needs to cover every category. Members should focus on the aspects relevant to their experience.

**4. Review Rating System**

Members may rate operators based on their overall experience.

We recommend a five-star rating system:

- ★★★★★ Excellent
- ★★★★☆ Very Good
- ★★★☆☆ Average
- ★★☆☆☆ Poor
- ★☆☆☆☆ Very Poor

Members should provide a short explanation supporting their rating.

Ratings should reflect genuine experiences and must not be manipulated through multiple accounts, coordinated voting or paid incentives.

**5. Deposits and Withdrawals**

Payment experiences are particularly important when evaluating gambling operators.

Members are encouraged to explain:

- Whether deposits were credited correctly.
- Whether withdrawals were processed within the stated timeframe.
- Whether additional verification was requested.
- Whether withdrawal limits or fees were applied.
- Whether any balance was restricted, withheld or confiscated.

Where a withdrawal dispute exists, members should provide relevant evidence where possible.

Sensitive financial information must always be removed before publication.

**6. Evidence and Supporting Information**

Reviews may include supporting material such as:

- Screenshots of transactions.
- Relevant correspondence with customer support.
- Bet settlement records.
- Bonus terms and conditions.
- Withdrawal confirmations.
- Other documentation supporting the review.

Members must not publish passwords, private keys, identity documents or sensitive personal information.

Moderators may request additional information where a review contains serious allegations or appears misleading.

**7. Fake Reviews and Rating Manipulation**

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- Fabricating personal experiences.
- Publishing reviews on behalf of someone else without disclosure.
- Creating multiple accounts to manipulate ratings.
- Paying members to publish favourable reviews without clear disclosure.
- Organising coordinated negative review campaigns.
- Posting fake testimonials or fabricated screenshots.
- Impersonating customers or casino representatives.

Reviews found to involve deliberate manipulation may be removed, and accounts involved may face moderation action.

**8. Affiliate Links and Commercial Relationships**

Members may have commercial relationships with casinos, sportsbooks and other gambling businesses.

These relationships must be disclosed.

If a member receives affiliate commissions, sponsorship payments, free bets, bonuses or other benefits from an operator they are reviewing, this must be clearly stated.

Members must not present paid promotional material as independent customer feedback.

Commercial relationships do not automatically invalidate a review, but readers must be able to understand any potential conflict of interest.

**9. Right of Reply for Casinos and Sportsbooks**

Operators are welcome to participate in reviews and respond to feedback.

Official representatives may:

- Clarify policies and terms.
- Respond to customer complaints.
- Provide additional information.
- Correct demonstrably inaccurate statements.
- Explain decisions relating to individual cases.

Representatives should use verified company accounts wherever possible.

Operators must not threaten members, harass reviewers or demand the removal of legitimate criticism.

**10. Independence and Commercial Neutrality**

Pitchside Forum may have advertising, affiliate and sponsorship relationships with gambling operators.

These commercial relationships will not determine review ratings or moderation decisions.

- Advertisers cannot purchase positive reviews.
- Sponsors cannot demand favourable ratings.
- Negative reviews will not be removed simply because an operator is a commercial partner.
- Operators must be treated according to the same standards as everyone else.
- Paid promotional content must be clearly distinguished from independent reviews.

Our commitment is to maintain an independent and credible review environment.

**11. Moderation of Reviews**

Moderators may review content to ensure compliance with community rules.

They may:

- Remove fake or manipulated reviews.
- Request clarification or supporting evidence.
- Remove personal or confidential information.
- Correct categorisation or formatting issues.
- Merge duplicate reviews.
- Remove abusive or defamatory content where appropriate.

Moderators should not alter a member's genuine opinion simply because it is critical of an operator.

Serious allegations should be distinguished from established facts and clearly presented as allegations where they have not been independently verified.

**12. Updating Reviews**

Members may update their reviews when their experience changes.

For example:

- A previously unresolved withdrawal is eventually paid.
- Customer support resolves an outstanding complaint.
- An operator changes its policies.
- A previously positive experience deteriorates.

Members are encouraged to update their reviews rather than leave outdated information uncorrected.

Original reviews should remain distinguishable from subsequent updates.

**13. Review Verification**

Pitchside Forum may introduce a review verification system to distinguish between:

- Verified customer experiences.
- Reviews supported by submitted evidence.
- Unverified member opinions.
- Independent information and editorial assessments.

Verification does not mean Pitchside Forum guarantees the accuracy of every statement. It indicates that certain checks or supporting evidence have been reviewed.

**14. Disclaimer**

Reviews represent the opinions and experiences of individual members.

Pitchside Forum does not guarantee that every review is accurate, complete or representative of an operator's overall service.

Members should conduct their own research before choosing a casino or sportsbook.

Where appropriate, serious disputes should also be referred to the relevant gambling regulator or dispute resolution authority.

**15. Our Commitment**

We believe players should be able to share honest experiences without fear of censorship or retaliation.

We also believe businesses should have a fair opportunity to respond to criticism and correct inaccurate information.

Our objective is not to promote or attack individual operators. It is to create a transparent environment where genuine experiences, evidence and fair discussion help members make better-informed decisions.

**Honest reviews. Independent ratings. Transparency for everyone.**

**The Pitchside Forum Team**
