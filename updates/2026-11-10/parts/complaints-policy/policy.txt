Pitchside Forum welcomes honest discussions and complaints about online casinos, sportsbooks and gambling platforms.

Players should have a place to share their experiences, raise concerns, warn others and seek responses from operators.

Our objective is to provide a fair and transparent environment where both players and casino representatives have an opportunity to present their side.

**1. What Can Be Reported?**

Members may submit complaints concerning:

- Delayed or refused withdrawals.
- Account closures or restrictions.
- Confiscated or withheld balances.
- KYC and verification disputes.
- Bonus terms and wagering requirements.
- Bet settlements and disputed results.
- Suspicious account activity or alleged unfair treatment.
- Unresponsive customer support.
- Other disputes involving casinos or sportsbooks.

Complaints should relate to genuine experiences or identifiable concerns.

**2. How to Submit a Complaint**

To help moderators and casino representatives understand the issue, members should include as much relevant information as possible.

A complaint should ideally contain:

- Casino or sportsbook name.
- Username or account identifier (never publish passwords or sensitive credentials).
- Date the issue occurred.
- Amount involved, where applicable.
- A clear explanation of what happened.
- Relevant correspondence or supporting evidence.
- Any steps already taken to resolve the issue directly with the operator.

Members should avoid posting unnecessary personal information, financial account details or unredacted identity documents.

**3. Evidence and Supporting Information**

Members are encouraged to provide supporting evidence where available.

This may include:

- Screenshots of relevant transactions.
- Withdrawal or deposit records.
- Correspondence with customer support.
- Relevant terms and conditions.
- Bet IDs or transaction references.
- Other documentation that helps explain the dispute.

Sensitive information must be redacted before publication.

Moderators may request additional information if a complaint is unclear or requires clarification.

**4. Opportunity for Casino Representatives to Respond**

Where possible, Pitchside Forum will invite the relevant casino or sportsbook to respond to complaints.

Representatives should:

- Identify themselves using an authorised or verified company account.
- Address the specific issues raised.
- Provide relevant explanations or evidence where appropriate.
- Communicate professionally and avoid personal attacks.

Operators are welcome to defend their decisions, correct inaccurate information and explain their policies.

However, they cannot demand the removal of legitimate criticism simply because it is unfavourable.

**5. Fairness and Impartiality**

Pitchside Forum aims to remain independent and impartial.

The following principles apply:

- Every complaint should be assessed on its merits.
- Members are entitled to share genuine negative experiences.
- Casinos and sportsbooks are entitled to respond.
- Sponsorship, advertising or affiliate relationships do not influence complaint decisions.
- No business can purchase a favourable outcome or have legitimate complaints suppressed.
- Moderators should distinguish between documented facts, personal experiences and unverified allegations.

A complaint does not automatically establish wrongdoing by an operator, just as an operator's denial does not automatically invalidate a player's complaint.

**6. Prohibited Complaint Behaviour**

The complaints section must not be used to:

- Fabricate disputes or supporting evidence.
- Publish knowingly false allegations.
- Impersonate casino representatives or players.
- Threaten, blackmail or harass operators or other members.
- Publish private or confidential information without permission.
- Coordinate fake complaints or manipulate discussions.
- Use complaints as a means of demanding unrelated payments or benefits.

Legitimate criticism and strong opinions are permitted, provided they are not accompanied by prohibited conduct.

**7. Complaint Status and Outcomes**

Moderators may assign a status to complaints to help members understand their progress.

Suggested statuses include:

- **Open:** Complaint submitted and awaiting review.
- **Under Review:** Additional information or clarification is being assessed.
- **Awaiting Operator Response:** The casino has been invited to respond.
- **Operator Responded:** The operator has provided its position.
- **Resolved:** The member confirms that the issue has been resolved.
- **Unresolved:** The parties have not reached a satisfactory outcome.
- **Closed:** The discussion has concluded or no further action is possible.

A complaint should not be marked resolved solely because an operator claims to have addressed it. Where possible, the complainant should confirm the outcome.

**8. Moderation of Complaints**

Moderators may:

- Request clarification or supporting evidence.
- Remove sensitive personal information.
- Edit or remove content that violates forum rules.
- Merge duplicate complaints.
- Invite relevant operators to respond.
- Close discussions that have become abusive or unproductive.

Moderators are not responsible for independently investigating every allegation or determining legal liability.

Complaints should not be removed merely because an operator disagrees with the member's account.

**9. Casino Representatives and Verification**

Casino representatives are welcome to participate in discussions and respond to player complaints.

Where possible, official representatives should request verification through the administration team.

Verified representatives may receive a visible verification badge.

Verification confirms the representative's claimed association with an operator; it does not mean Pitchside Forum endorses the company or guarantees the accuracy of its statements.

Impersonating an official representative is strictly prohibited.

**10. Limitations and Disclaimer**

Pitchside Forum is an independent discussion community, not a gambling regulator, licensing authority, court or alternative dispute resolution body.

We cannot:

- Force a casino to release funds.
- Reverse bets or account decisions.
- Access private casino accounts.
- Guarantee that a complaint will be resolved.
- Provide binding legal or regulatory decisions.

Members with unresolved disputes should also consider contacting the relevant gambling regulator, licensing authority or appropriate dispute resolution service.

**11. Appeals and Further Review**

Members who believe a complaint has been unfairly moderated may contact the administration team.

Casino representatives may also raise concerns about impersonation, fabricated evidence or violations of forum rules.

All requests should be assessed impartially and in accordance with the community guidelines.

**12. Our Commitment**

Pitchside Forum believes that transparency and accountability benefit both players and responsible operators.

Players should be able to speak openly about their experiences, and businesses should have a fair opportunity to respond.

We will not protect a casino's reputation at the expense of legitimate player complaints, nor will we allow the complaints section to become a platform for fabricated allegations or harassment.

**Honest experiences. Fair discussion. Accountability for everyone.**

**The Pitchside Forum Team**
