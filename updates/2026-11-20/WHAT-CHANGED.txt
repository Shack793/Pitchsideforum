20 November 2026 update (install with:  sudo bash apply.sh  from inside this folder)

New in this update:

- Faster page start: every page now tells the browser to download its scripts at the same time as the
  page itself, instead of only after the whole page (with its data) has arrived. The list of scripts is
  read from the page, so it always matches whatever is installed. Part: script-preload
  (app/ops/zz-script-preload.php; loaded automatically with the other app/ops files).
- Sports page organised by sport: /sports shows Football first with its competitions grouped by region,
  then a compact directory of the other sports (basketball, American football, tennis, cricket, rugby,
  baseball, ice hockey, motorsport, combat sports). Each sport has its own page at /sports/<sport> with
  tidy competition cards (name first; type, sport, country and season small) and the latest discussion.
  Bare counts and the "Players and fighters..." line are gone. Part: sports-dir
  (public/assets/sports-dir.1.js, loaded from app/shell.html after league-boards.1.js).

Everything from the earlier updates is included in this installer too, so it is safe to run on a site
that has any of the earlier updates missing. Anything already installed is left as it is.
