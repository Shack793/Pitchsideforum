**1. Introduction**

The Pitchside Marketplace provides members with a place to buy, sell, trade and advertise legitimate products and services.

We welcome individuals, businesses and service providers who wish to engage with the community.

To maintain a safe and trustworthy marketplace, all members must follow these guidelines.

**2. Who Can Use the Marketplace?**

- Registered Pitchside Forum members may participate in marketplace discussions.
- Members must provide accurate information when advertising products or services.
- New accounts may be subject to additional restrictions before creating listings.
- Members with a history of scams, fraud or marketplace abuse may lose access to the marketplace.

Pitchside Forum reserves the right to restrict marketplace access where necessary to protect the community.

**3. Creating a Listing**

Every listing should include:

- A clear and descriptive title.
- An accurate description of the product or service.
- The asking price or pricing structure.
- Relevant photographs or supporting information, where applicable.
- Payment methods accepted.
- Delivery or fulfilment arrangements.
- Any important conditions or limitations.

Listings must be genuine, accurate and kept up to date.

Do not advertise products or services you cannot provide.

**4. Buying and Selling**

Members are responsible for exercising caution when conducting transactions.

Before completing a purchase:

- Check the seller's account history and reputation.
- Ask questions about the product or service.
- Agree on the price and payment terms.
- Clarify delivery and refund arrangements.
- Keep relevant transaction records.

Sellers should accurately describe what they are offering and fulfil their obligations as agreed.

**5. Cryptocurrency Transactions**

Cryptocurrency-related trading and services are permitted where lawful.

However:

- Clearly specify the cryptocurrency and network involved.
- Confirm wallet addresses and transaction details before sending funds.
- Never share private keys or recovery phrases.
- Be cautious of unusually attractive exchange rates or offers.
- Do not promote fraudulent investment schemes or deceptive giveaways.
- Do not misrepresent payment confirmations or transaction records.

Cryptocurrency transactions are generally difficult or impossible to reverse. Members should take particular care before transferring funds.

**6. Services and Freelance Work**

Members may advertise legitimate services, including:

- Web development and programming.
- Graphic design and digital marketing.
- Sports data and analytics services.
- Writing and content creation.
- Technical support and consultancy.
- Other lawful professional services.

Service providers must clearly explain what they offer, their pricing and expected delivery times.

Do not advertise services you are unable or unwilling to deliver.

**7. Prohibited Listings**

The following are not permitted:

- Stolen or counterfeit goods.
- Illegal products or services.
- Fraudulent investment schemes.
- Phishing services, malware or malicious software.
- Stolen accounts or compromised credentials.
- Fake documents or identity-related services intended to facilitate fraud.
- Prohibited financial or gambling services.
- Misleading offers or deceptive promotions.

The administration team may remove listings that present a risk to members or violate applicable rules.

**8. Reputation and Feedback**

Members are encouraged to share honest feedback about their marketplace experiences.

- Feedback should relate to genuine transactions.
- Do not publish fabricated testimonials.
- Do not manipulate seller ratings.
- Do not create multiple accounts to artificially improve or damage reputations.
- Disclose any financial or personal relationship that could influence a review.

Sellers must not threaten members for leaving honest negative feedback.

**9. Scams and Disputes**

If you believe you have been scammed or misled:

1. Contact the other party and attempt to resolve the issue.
2. Preserve relevant messages, transaction records and evidence.
3. Report the matter to the moderation team.
4. Provide a clear explanation of what happened.

Moderators may review the report, request additional information and take appropriate action against accounts involved in suspected misconduct.

Where appropriate, members should also contact their payment provider or relevant authorities.

**10. Advertising and Promotional Activity**

Commercial promotions must follow the Pitchside Advertising & Promotional Guidelines.

- Do not flood marketplace sections with repetitive listings.
- Do not post advertisements in unrelated discussions.
- Clearly disclose affiliate links and commercial relationships.
- Do not use multiple accounts to promote the same service.
- Do not send unsolicited promotional private messages.

Businesses seeking prominent or recurring advertising may contact the administration team regarding commercial opportunities.

**11. Verification and Trusted Sellers**

Pitchside Forum may introduce optional seller verification and reputation features.

These may include:

- Verified business badges.
- Seller feedback and transaction history.
- Account age and activity indicators.
- Verified representative status.
- Additional checks for higher-risk marketplace categories.

Verification confirms only the information or checks specified by Pitchside Forum. It does not guarantee that a seller is trustworthy or that every transaction will be successful.

**12. Moderator Intervention**

The moderation team may:

- Remove misleading or prohibited listings.
- Request additional information.
- Restrict marketplace access.
- Issue warnings or suspend accounts.
- Permanently ban members involved in serious fraud or repeated abuse.

Serious allegations should be assessed fairly, and members should have an opportunity to provide relevant information.

**13. Important Disclaimer**

Pitchside Forum provides a platform for members to advertise and discuss products and services.

We do not act as an escrow service, payment processor, broker or guarantor of private transactions.

Members are responsible for assessing the risks of their transactions and complying with applicable laws.

Pitchside Forum cannot guarantee delivery, product quality, payment recovery or successful dispute resolution.

**14. Final Word**

A successful marketplace depends on trust, transparency and responsible participation.

We encourage members to trade honestly, provide accurate information and treat one another fairly.

Help us build a marketplace where legitimate businesses and community members can interact with confidence.

**Trade responsibly. Be transparent. Protect yourself and the community.**

**The Pitchside Forum Team**
