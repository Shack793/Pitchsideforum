19 November 2026 update (install with:  sudo bash apply.sh  from inside this folder)

New in this update:

- Tidier review pages: pros and cons move under the Safety Index card in two columns, the repeated
  score badge is removed from that card, and phones stack the lists. Part: review-tidy.
- Sports simplified:
  * "FIFA World Cup" is now "International" (league and board; same address).
  * League pages lose the Teams and Betting tabs; team pages lose Betting; match pages lose the
    "Betting discussion" box.
  * Football has no team pages: one section per league; old club links go to their league, and
    football clubs are left out of the sitemap.
  * The prediction leaderboard no longer shows sample member names.
  Part: sports-tidy.
- Crypto section in three boards: Cryptocurrency Discussion, Crypto Casinos & Sportsbooks and
  Crypto Trading & Investments. Topics from the old coin boards move to Cryptocurrency Discussion,
  and old coin-board addresses forward there. Board moderators are kept. Part: crypto-boards.
- Marketplace removed from the site: menu link hidden, section switched off, escrow off, and the
  Marketplace boards closed so only admins can still see old posts. Nothing is deleted (the old
  settings are kept in MARKET_REMOVED). Part: market-remove.
- Tennis in one place: one "Tennis" board with sub-boards for ATP Tour, WTA Tour, Grand Slams, ITF
  and Davis Cup & Billie Jean King Cup, and one Tennis entry on the Sports page. Old /leagues/atp
  and /leagues/wta addresses forward to /leagues/tennis; topics, follows and match links move with
  them. Part: tennis.
- SEO check-up:
  * Pages that don't exist answer 404 (with noindex) instead of an indexable copy of the homepage
    (unknown addresses, leagues, teams, tags, closed boards and sections).
  * Real search titles and descriptions for /sports, /crypto, /gambling, /community, /forum,
    /blacklist, the info pages and the tag pages (added in Admin -> SEO only where missing).
  * One address per page: casinos/sportsbooks at the address the site links to, /teams and /leagues
    -> /sports, /reviews -> /gambling, no trailing "/" copies.
  * Sports league descriptions no longer say "betting tips".
  Part: seo-check.
- Safety Index weights safety fix: licence 15% and member reviews 25% are still set, but if the
  weights were changed since (for example the admin rating share) the existing weights are kept
  instead of being reset. Part: weights.

The installer still runs every earlier part first, so it is safe to run on a site that has any of
the earlier updates missing. Anything already installed is left as it is.
